## Professional Self-Assessment

My experience in the Computer Science program at Southern New Hampshire University has given me a broad technical foundation in software development, algorithms and data structures, databases, networking, and cybersecurity. Throughout the program, I have grown from focusing primarily on whether a solution works to considering its efficiency, maintainability, security, and value to the people who use it.

My coursework has helped me develop skills across several areas of computer science. Through software engineering, I learned to design, test, troubleshoot, and improve applications while emphasizing readable and maintainable code. My work with algorithms and data structures taught me to consider efficiency and the trade-offs involved when selecting data structures and implementing searching and sorting operations. Database projects strengthened my understanding of storing, organizing, and retrieving information, while my networking and cybersecurity coursework developed my awareness of access control, network security, least privilege, and potential vulnerabilities.

Creating this ePortfolio allowed me to revisit earlier projects and apply the knowledge I gained later in the program. The three enhancements presented below demonstrate my growth in Software Design and Engineering, Algorithms and Data Structures, and Databases. Together, they show my ability to evaluate existing solutions, identify opportunities for improvement, implement meaningful technical changes, and communicate the reasoning behind those decisions.

As I move forward professionally, I am particularly interested in networking and infrastructure. My background in programming, databases, cybersecurity, and computer science gives me a broader understanding of the systems that operate across modern networks. I plan to continue developing these skills while building secure, reliable, and efficient computing environments.
